# Welcome to my new site

Hey! 👋

If you've been here before, you'll probably notice that things look a little different.

I've rebuilt my personal site from the ground up to make it feel more like **my own space on the web** rather than just another portfolio.

It's still a work in progress, but I wanted to finally put it out there.

## What you'll find here

This site is mostly a place for me to collect the things I'm working on and the things I find interesting.

### Projects

A lot of my time goes into random projects, experiments, and tools that I decide to build because I think they're interesting.

Some of them turn into actual applications, some get abandoned halfway through, and some exist purely because I wanted to figure out how something works.

I'll be using the projects section to document some of them and link back to their repositories.

### Writing

The blog is where I'll put longer-form stuff.

That could be a technical write-up, something I learned while building a project, a Linux-related experiment, or just a deep dive into something I found interesting.

I don't really want this to become a "how to become a developer" type of blog. It's more of a place to document things I've actually done and learned along the way.

### About me

There's also an about page if you want to know a little more about me and the things I'm interested in.

I'm a self-taught developer who enjoys building things, messing around with computers, and learning by breaking things.

I spend a lot of my free time coding, playing games, listening to music, watching anime, and generally tinkering with whatever happens to catch my attention.

## The site itself

The site is built with **React and TypeScript**, with Vite handling the development and build tooling.

Styling is intentionally kept fairly lightweight with regular CSS and design tokens rather than relying on a large utility framework.

The goal is to keep the site relatively simple while still having enough structure to make it feel polished.

It's also hosted using **Cloudflare Pages**, with the source available on GitHub.

## Why rebuild it?

Honestly, the previous version had accumulated a lot of things that I didn't really use anymore.

It started as a place to experiment with different ideas, and over time it became a collection of old components, abandoned features, and design decisions that no longer really represented what I wanted.

So rather than continuously patching it, I decided to start over.

This version is smaller, cleaner, and much easier for me to change.

And that's probably the most important part.

## What's next?

I don't have a strict roadmap for the site.

I'll probably add things as I need them and experiment with new ideas along the way.

Some things I'd like to work on include:

- More project write-ups
- Technical blog posts
- Development notes and experiments
- More information about my projects
- Improving the site itself as I use it

There will probably be plenty of unfinished ideas along the way too.

That's kind of the point.
